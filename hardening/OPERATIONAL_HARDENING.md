# Streetcraft Operational Hardening 1.6.4

SVS 1.6.4 reduces small but destructive drift during image reinterpretation.

It adds five operational controls:

1. **Semantic Text Lock (STL)** — illegible or partial text cannot become invented semantic content.
2. **Material Intensity Delta (MID)** — aging, grime, wetness, corrosion and decay cannot silently intensify beyond source/mode authority.
3. **Occlusion Locks (OL)** — hidden geometry stays unresolved unless supported by multi-view evidence or explicit reconstruction authority.
4. **Compact Generation Contract (CGC)** — every generation resolves into an explicit compact contract before rendering.
5. **Micro-Drift Critic (MDC)** — catches small geometry, text, material and identity deviations that can survive a coarse visual review.

These controls do not change Visual Canon authority. They operationalize existing Streetcraft principles.

# Documentary Evidence V1

Documentary Evidence answers: how did the represented world plausibly behave? It does not answer: how should Streetcraft transform an image?

Primary evidence domains established during SVS 1.1:
- ordinary architecture and storefronts
- signage accumulation and ghost signage
- working/service streets
- elevated/transit edge
- rain, snow, asphalt and material interaction
- urban residue and causal density
- small commerce and street routine
- temporal layering with different persistence
- documentary friction and imperfect composition
- industrial adjacency and vacant urban land

Use documentary material to constrain plausibility, period, material behavior and environmental causality. Do not use it to import geographic identity, literal signage or composition into an unrelated source.

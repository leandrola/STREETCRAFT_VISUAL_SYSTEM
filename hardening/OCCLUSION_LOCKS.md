# Occlusion Locks (OL)

Occluded regions are not blank creative space.

## Lock states

- `LOCKED_UNKNOWN` — hidden content must remain unresolved.
- `LOCKED_RELATIONSHIP` — the hidden object may remain unseen, but its known relationship must be preserved.
- `MULTIVIEW_CONSTRAINED` — another authoritative view supports limited reconstruction.
- `EXPLICIT_RECONSTRUCTION` — the user/mode explicitly authorizes reconstruction.

## Rules

A single-view source never authorizes detailed invention behind:
- parked vehicles
- people
- signs
- columns
- vegetation
- deep shadows
- frame edges

Clean Plate removal may reconstruct only the minimum continuity necessary. It is not permission to redesign the facade, storefront, sidewalk or street.

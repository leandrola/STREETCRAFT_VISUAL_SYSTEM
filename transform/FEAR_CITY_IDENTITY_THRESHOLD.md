# Fear City Identity Threshold

Fear City routing is identity-based, never resemblance-based.

## CONFIRMED
Route to `T06 + VP03 + CG-FC` when Fear City is explicitly identified, or when a known source/multi-view set supports at least:
- one structural anchor, and
- one identity anchor.

## PROBABLE
One strong anchor plus contextual resemblance is insufficient for automatic VP03 routing.

## UNRESOLVED
Miniature/diorama material that resembles Fear City but lacks enough authored-world anchors remains unresolved.

## NOT_APPLICABLE
Real-world or unrelated authored material follows normal routing.

## Traits that never confirm Fear City alone
New York resemblance, brick, graffiti, elevated steel, grime, period vehicles, miniature appearance, night photography or general Fear City mood.

# Provenance and Trust

Pinterest is a discovery/intake source, not automatically a historical authority.

## Provenance states
P0 UNKNOWN_ORIGIN
P1 PINTEREST_ONLY
P2 ATTRIBUTED_SOURCE
P3 ORIGINAL_SOURCE_RESOLVED
P4 PRIMARY_ARCHIVE_OR_DOCUMENTED_SOURCE

## Trust implications
P0/P1 can support visual-form discovery but should not establish precise historical claims.
P2 may support moderate contextual claims.
P3/P4 can support stronger period/regional/documentary assertions when content agrees.

## Historical metadata confidence
Date, city, business identity and photographer attribution must each have independent confidence. Do not infer one merely from another.

## Duplicate / derivative handling
Store perceptual/source fingerprints to identify:
- exact duplicate
- crop
- colorized copy
- contrast/scan variant
- repost of same underlying photograph

Prefer one canonical archive record with linked variants when possible.

# Visual Profiles

Profiles define visual behavior. They do not replace source authority or Transformation Modes.

## VP00 STREETCRAFT CORE
Streetcraft's broad architectural interpretation profile.

Camera:
- Human eye-level or slightly low.
- CG-A or CG-B when transformation is permitted.
- CG-S under source-locked conservative reinterpretation.
- No mandatory frontalization.
- No aerial overview by default.

Visual behavior:
- Architecture dominant.
- Causal imperfection, surface memory and temporal layering.
- Atmospheric restraint and regional coherence.
- Source identity preserved.
- No AI beautification.
- Vehicles/people absent by default in generative or reconstructive outputs, but T01 preserves source-contained human/vehicle evidence unless removal is authorized.

Use VP00 when the task should feel unmistakably Streetcraft without imposing the stronger heritage constraints of RDR Classic or RDR 2.0.

## VP01 RDR CLASSIC
North American mid-century urban architecture with diorama readability and realistic spatial depth.

Camera:
- CG-A preferred.
- CG-B allowed for corners, industrial blocks, side planes and urban depth.
- Human eye-level or slightly low.
- Frontal-oblique is preferred over strict frontal orthogonality.
- One dominant vanishing point with a weak second convergence is ideal when source geometry supports it.
- Controlled two-point perspective is valid.
- CG-S remains mandatory when source preservation outranks profile transformation.

Visual behavior:
- Architecture protagonist.
- Restrained palette.
- Reduced activity.
- Realistic depth with residual model/diorama readability.
- Avoid theatrical wide-angle exaggeration.
- Vehicles/people absent by default unless source/mode authority requires preservation.

## VP02 RDR 2.0
Strict architectural-immersion heritage profile for North American/USA urban scenes, especially 1960s–1970s.

Camera:
- Human eye-level to slightly low.
- CG-A is primary.
- CG-B is explicitly permitted when the architecture is read through a corner, side plane or deeper street-level relationship.
- Strict yaw≈0° is retired as a mandatory requirement.
- Strict one-point frontal perspective is retired as a mandatory requirement.
- One-point frontal remains a valid special case when naturally supported by the scene.
- The preferred reading is nearly architectural elevation with real-world spatial depth, not a flat facade diagram.
- Aerial and elevated overview remain forbidden by default.
- CG-S still outranks the profile under source-locked T01.

Visual behavior:
- Architecture dominant and immersive.
- People and vehicles absent by default.
- Minimal activity.
- Historical target remains North America/USA 1960s–1970s.
- Mid-rise brick, rooftop signs, water towers, Art Deco verticals and industrial/commercial character remain compatible lineage signals, never mandatory object lists.
- The scene should read as a plausible real urban photograph while preserving the handcrafted/model-derived lineage of RDR 2.0.
- Do not use cinematic mood, decay or props to compensate for weak composition.

## VP03 FEAR CITY
Fear City is an authored physical world and uses its own camera authority.

Camera:
- CG-FC default.
- Preserve authored geometry and the evidentiary relationship between camera and model.
- Do not inherit VP00/VP01/VP02 camera preferences automatically.
- Any camera translation must support world-scale plausibility without redesigning the physical authorship.

Visual behavior:
- Model authorship absolute.
- Layout and facade identity absolute.
- Signage identity strong.
- Material intention strong.
- Apparent scale, atmospheric depth and lighting realism transformable.
- Background continuity inferable only within evidence.

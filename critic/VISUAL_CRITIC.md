P1 moderate alteration = MAJOR DELTA
P2 small variation = ACCEPTABLE DELTA
P3 intended transformation = AUTHORIZED DELTA
P4 removal = EXPECTED DELTA
P5 reconstruction = evaluate against evidence boundary

# Evidence Model

An archive image is not a monolithic authority. It contains domain-specific Evidence Units.

## Evidence Unit fields
- domain
- subject
- observation
- visual_location
- evidence_type
- confidence
- transfer_eligibility
- notes

An image may be GOLD-level evidence for one domain and weak for another.

## Authority principle
Archive evidence can constrain plausibility and material behavior. It cannot override stronger source evidence or silently define the subject of a transformation.

# Visual Grammar and Streetcraft Rules

## Core grammar
VG-01 Spatial Structure — clear massing, opening rhythm, ground relation and spatial reading.
VG-02 Human Camera — physically plausible observer; eye/street-level by default unless Mode/Profile says otherwise.
VG-03 Architectural Ordinariness — ordinary working/living/selling/storing/repair architecture over spectacle.
VG-04 Surface Memory — weathering must have physical causes and spatial logic.
VG-05 Temporal Layering — building, business, repair, signage and street ages may differ.
VG-06 Environmental Storytelling — secondary elements suggest use, not decoration.
VG-07 Atmospheric Restraint — cinematic only when physically plausible and source-compatible.
VG-08 Regional Specificity — known regional evidence outranks generic Americana.

## Camera Grammar — formal camera families

Camera is governed hierarchically:

SOURCE IDENTITY → TRANSFORMATION MODE → PROFILE → CAMERA FAMILY

Camera grammar must never destroy source identity under conservative modes. It constrains how Streetcraft interprets or reconstructs the scene when camera transformation is permitted.

### CG-A — Frontal-Oblique Architectural Immersion
Primary architectural camera family for VP00/VP01/VP02 when source/mode permit transformation.

- Apparent camera height: human eye-level to slightly low.
- View: frontal-oblique, close to architectural elevation but not forced orthographic/frontal.
- Perspective: one dominant vanishing point with a weak secondary convergence, or controlled two-point perspective.
- Architectural plane remains dominant.
- Enough side depth may be visible to create immersion and believable urban volume.
- Vertical convergence should remain controlled; dramatic upward tilt is forbidden unless source/task requires it.
- The scene should be capable of being mistaken for a real street-level urban photograph rather than a flattened backdrop.
- Vehicles and people are absent by default in generative/reconstructive use. Under T01 they are preserved when they are source evidence unless removal is explicitly authorized.

### CG-B — Street-Level Oblique Monumentality
Secondary architectural camera family for corners, side planes and deeper street geometry.

- Apparent camera height: human eye-level to slightly low.
- View: oblique at street level.
- Perspective: principally two-point.
- The observer should feel physically present in the street, facing a facade or corner with architectural monumentality.
- Architecture and urban fabric dominate the frame.
- Side depth may become stronger than in CG-A, but may not become wide-angle spectacle.
- Aerial, bird's-eye, drone-like and elevated overview readings are forbidden unless explicit source/mode authority requires them.
- Vehicles and people are absent by default in generative/reconstructive use. T01 source preservation still has higher authority.

### CG-S — Source-Locked Camera
Used when camera transformation would violate source identity or transformation intent.

- Preserve source camera height, yaw, pitch, crop logic and dominant convergence as closely as practical.
- Do not force CG-A or CG-B simply because a profile prefers them.
- T01 Faithful Reinterpretation defaults to CG-S unless the source already naturally belongs to CG-A or CG-B.
- CG-S is a preservation mechanism, not a visual style.

### CG-FC — Fear City Authored-View Camera
Used after Fear City auto-routing or explicit Fear City selection.

- Preserve the authored physical world and the evidentiary camera relationship to it.
- Correct or translate only capture/model scale breakers that are permitted under T06.
- Do not force RDR frontalization or generic two-point dramatization.
- Camera changes must increase world plausibility without redesigning authored geometry.
- Multi-view evidence may support a more coherent world-space reading, but never licenses new geometry absent from all views.

## Camera selection constraints

1. CG-A and CG-B are the normal transformable camera families for VP00/VP01/VP02.
2. One-point frontal perspective remains legal, but is no longer mandatory for VP02.
3. VP02 may use CG-B when corner geometry, side depth or urban massing materially contributes to the source.
4. Under conservative source-preserving work, CG-S outranks profile preference.
5. Fear City routing selects CG-FC by default.
6. Camera grammar does not authorize new architecture, new streets, new signage or new geographic identity.
7. Camera immersion must come from spatial reading, not atmospheric inflation.
8. Camera transformation must preserve the identity-bearing relationships of the source.
